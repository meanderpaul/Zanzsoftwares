# Privacy Policy — Strato Altus

**App:** Strato Altus  
**Package:** `com.zanz.StratoAltus`  
**Last updated:** August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Strato Altus (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Android app published on Google Play.

## 1. Summary

- Strato Altus is a hiking and peak-challenge app with accounts, hike logs, badges, leaderboards, and optional community photo features.
- We collect only what is needed to run those features.
- We do **not** sell your personal information.
- We do **not** use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
- You can delete your account in-app, or request help via email — see [Account & data deletion](/#/delete/strato-altus).

## 2. Information we collect

- **Account & profile** — first name, last name, email address, date of birth, username, password (stored as a secure hash), optional profile photo. Collected when you sign up or edit your profile.
- **Hike & activity logs** — peaks or places you log, dates, ratings, notes, trail details, verification status.
- **Photos you upload** — summit photos, contest entries, and profile avatars, only when you choose to attach or upload an image.
- **Approximate location** — GPS coordinates embedded in photo EXIF metadata, used to verify a summit when you submit a photo. We do not track your location in the background.
- **Community display data** — username and aggregated stats on leaderboards; contest photos shown with your username.
- **Device-local preferences** — login session identifiers, cached stats, distance units, height preference, offline hike queue. Cleared when you sign out (units/height prefs may remain).

## 3. How we use your information

- Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
- Record hikes, calculate challenge progress, badges, and leaderboard rankings.
- Host and display photos you submit for hike verification, avatars, or the community contest.
- Send transactional email (for example password reset) when you request it.
- Operate moderation and abuse-prevention features to keep the community safe.
- Deliver app updates through normal app store or in-app update channels.

## 4. How we share information

We do not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers. Limited sharing occurs only as needed to operate the service:

- **Other users** — your username, leaderboard stats, and contest photos you choose to submit may be visible in the app.
- **Service providers** — email delivery (for account recovery) and hosting infrastructure that stores app data.
- **Legal requirements** — we may disclose information if required by law or to protect users and the service.

## 5. Android permissions

- **Camera / photos** — to attach summit photos, contest entries, or a profile avatar.
- **Location (from photos)** — read from image EXIF when you upload a photo for verification; not continuous GPS tracking.
- **Internet** — to sync hikes, stats, and account data with our server.

## 6. Data retention & security

We retain your data while your account is active. Passwords are stored using industry-standard one-way hashing. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. When you delete your account, we remove your account record, hike logs, uploaded photos tied to your account (including files on the server where technically possible), and password-reset tokens.

## 7. Your choices

- Update profile information in **Settings → Profile**.
- Remove your profile photo in Settings.
- Sign out to clear session data stored on the device.
- Delete your account at any time — see [Account & data deletion](/#/delete/strato-altus).

## 8. Account deletion

You can delete your account **inside the app** without contacting support:

1. Open **Settings → Account Management → Delete account**.
2. Confirm with your password.

Deletion is permanent and removes server-side account data as described above. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account. You may also email **zanzsoftwares@gmail.com** to ask questions about deletion or data access. Full instructions: [Account & data deletion](/#/delete/strato-altus).

## 9. Children’s privacy

Strato Altus is not directed at children under 13. You must be at least **13 years old** to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. International users

Our service is operated from the United States. If you use the app from another country, your information may be processed and stored in the U.S.

## 11. Google Play Data safety

When completing the Play Console **Data safety** form, declare collection of: name, email, user IDs, date of birth, photos/videos, approximate location (from user-uploaded photos), and other user-generated content (hike logs). Mark data as collected for app functionality, not sold, and not used for advertising. Users can request account deletion in-app as described above and on [Account & data deletion](/#/delete/strato-altus).

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
