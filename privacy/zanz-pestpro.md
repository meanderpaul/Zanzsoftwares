# Privacy Policy — Zanz PestPro

**App:** Zanz PestPro (Windows desktop, Microsoft Store)  
**Publisher:** Zanz Software  
**Last updated:** October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Zanz PestPro (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. Zanz PestPro is business software for pest control companies: customers, service routes, bait stations, jobs, crew time clock, payroll, inventory and reports.

## 1. Summary

- Everything you enter is stored **only on your own computer**. We do **not** run servers that receive your business data.
- We do **not** create accounts, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.
- We do **not** sell, rent or share your information.
- Subscriptions are bought and billed by **Microsoft** through the Microsoft Store. We never see your payment details.

## 2. Information stored on your computer

To run your business, you may enter personal information about other people, such as:

- **Customers and leads** — names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, service history, notes, payment status and call logs.
- **Employees** — names, roles, pay and commission settings, time-clock punches, time-off requests and payroll records.
- **Inventory and sales** — items, quantities, vehicles, orders, deals and related notes.

This data is saved in local database files under the Windows ProgramData folder on your PC (for example `C:\ProgramData\Customer Manager\`). It is not uploaded to Zanz Software or anyone else. You are responsible for how you collect and use your customers’ and employees’ information, and for any legal obligations you have toward them.

## 3. Internet and network access

- **Microsoft Store licensing** — the App asks the Microsoft Store on your PC whether your Zanz PestPro subscription is active. Microsoft handles this under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). We receive no personal information from this check.
- **Your local network (optional)** — if you turn on phone access for Inventory or multi-desk sync for Sales, the App shares data **only with devices on your own local network** that you connect. This data does not leave your network.
- **Links you click** — opening a Store or website link launches your browser or the Microsoft Store app.

## 4. Payments

Subscription purchases, renewals and cancellations are handled entirely by Microsoft. We receive standard sales reports from Microsoft that do not include your payment card details.

## 5. Children’s privacy

The App is business software for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your computer until you delete it. You can delete individual records inside the App, export your data to Excel, or remove all data by uninstalling the App and deleting its folder in ProgramData. Because we never receive your data, we cannot recover it for you — keep your own backups.

## 7. Security

Your data is protected by the security of your Windows PC and account. Use a Windows password, keep Windows updated, and back up your data regularly.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
