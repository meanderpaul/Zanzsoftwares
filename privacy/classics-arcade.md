# Privacy Policy — Classics Arcade

**App:** Classics Arcade  
**Package:** `com.zanzarcade.app`  
**Last updated:** July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Classics Arcade (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Android app published on Google Play.

## 1. Summary

- We do **not** create accounts, and we do **not** ask for your name, email, or phone number.
- Game progress and high scores are stored **only on your device**.
- The App shows ads through **Google AdMob**, which may collect device and advertising identifiers as described below.

## 2. Information we store on your device

The App saves gameplay data locally (for example high scores, campaign progress, and similar preferences) using on-device storage. This data stays on your device unless you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Play Store version of Classics Arcade uses Google AdMob to show interstitial advertisements. Google and its partners may collect and process information such as:

- Advertising ID / device identifiers
- IP address and approximate location derived from IP
- Device and app technical information
- Ad interaction and performance data

This information is used to deliver, measure, and improve ads according to Google’s policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Learn more:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [AdMob & privacy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

On Android you can reset or limit your advertising ID in system settings (typically under Privacy / Google / Ads).

## 4. Internet access

The App requests Internet permission to load and display ads. Core single-player gameplay does not require you to create an online account.

## 5. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience casual game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party advertising services, contact us and we will help address the issue as appropriate.

## 6. Data retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Advertising data retained by Google is governed by Google’s own retention practices.

## 7. Your choices

- Clear app storage or uninstall to remove local scores/progress.
- Adjust Android advertising / ad personalization settings.
- Opt out of personalized ads where offered by Google/AdMob.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
